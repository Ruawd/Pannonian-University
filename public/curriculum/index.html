Danube Systems

Regional Evidence & Pannonian Systems

Credits
6 ECTS
Academic Level
Foundation
Field Evidence Quota
42 mapped sources
Academic Lab Affiliate
Danube Evidence Studio

Research Connection

Connected to PU's Danube Basin Civic Atlas, a living research map of municipal services, agricultural zones, wetland risks, and public data gaps.

Learning Outcomes

  • Build a regional evidence brief from public datasets and field notes.
  • Read Serbian Danube planning documents with clear limits and assumptions.
  • Present a map-backed civic question to a faculty review panel.

Chronological Syllabus (4-week intensive modules)

  1. 01
    Regional Systems Orientation

    Pannonian geography, settlement patterns, institutions, and evidence ethics.

  2. 02
    Public Dataset Recovery

    National statistics, municipal budgets, hydrological records, and metadata quality checks.

  3. 03
    Field Question Design

    Turning a civic issue into a defensible research question and sampling plan.

  4. 04
    Evidence Brief Studio

    A concise dossier with map, method note, uncertainty label, and public-facing conclusion.

Assessment Pattern

Evidence brief 35%, dataset notebook 25%, field memo 20%, oral defense 20%.

6 ECTS

Core Exploration

Academic Lab Affiliate
AgriTech Remote Sensing Lab

Research Connection

Uses PU field plots near Novi Sad to compare Sentinel-2 vegetation indices, UAV imagery, and farmer-reported irrigation stress.

Learning Outcomes

  • Process remote sensing imagery into an agronomic interpretation.
  • Compare satellite and UAV signals without overstating precision.
  • Translate climate risk evidence into farm and policy recommendations.

Chronological Syllabus (4-week intensive modules)

  1. 01
    Vegetation Indices and Ground Truth

    NDVI, NDRE, soil moisture proxies, and the limits of spectral interpretation.

  2. 02
    UAV Mission Planning

    Flight planning, image stitching, control points, and field safety procedures.

  3. 03
    Irrigation Stress Detection

    Temporal comparison of crop vigor, heat exposure, and water availability.

  4. 04
    Decision Memo Lab

    A farm-facing advisory note with maps, uncertainty, and recommended next measurements.

Assessment Pattern

Imagery notebook 30%, field protocol 20%, decision memo 30%, peer critique 20%.

Academic Lab Affiliate
Water Futures Lab

Research Connection

Feeds the Water Futures Lab's groundwater risk archive for irrigation communities along the Danube and Tisa corridors.

Learning Outcomes

  • Execute a defensible water sampling chain from field site to lab record.
  • Interpret salinity, nitrate, and conductivity indicators for irrigation risk.
  • Prepare public communication that is accurate without being alarmist.

Chronological Syllabus (4-week intensive modules)

  1. 01
    Sampling Chain and Site Selection

    Field logs, bottles, preservation, duplicates, and location metadata.

  2. 02
    Water Chemistry Interpretation

    Conductivity, salinity, nitrate, pH, and threshold comparison.

  3. 03
    Aquifer Risk Mapping

    Combining sample evidence, land use, and irrigation dependence.

  4. 04
    Community Risk Note

    A public-facing note with clear evidence levels and next-step monitoring advice.

Assessment Pattern

Lab notebook 30%, risk map 25%, methods audit 20%, community note 25%.

Academic Lab Affiliate
Regional Growth Observatory

Research Connection

Supports the Regional Growth Observatory's work on logistics corridors, municipal finance stress, and circular bioeconomy investment.

Learning Outcomes

  • Model regional growth tradeoffs using transparent assumptions.
  • Evaluate public finance and logistics constraints in cross-border industry.
  • Write an investment memo that weighs growth, resilience, and social risk.

Chronological Syllabus (4-week intensive modules)

  1. 01
    Regional Accounts and Productive Capacity

    Employment, sector mix, freight routes, and local fiscal structure.

  2. 02
    Circular Bioeconomy Finance

    Agricultural byproducts, investment cases, lifecycle claims, and risk controls.

  3. 03
    Cross-Border Industry Scenarios

    Scenario modeling for Serbia-EU trade, logistics, and infrastructure constraints.

  4. 04
    Responsible Growth Memo

    A board-ready memo with assumptions, sensitivity checks, and equity considerations.

Assessment Pattern

Scenario model 30%, finance memo 30%, seminar brief 20%, oral defense 20%.

Academic Lab Affiliate
Borderlands Oral History Archive

Research Connection

Contributes to PU's Borderlands Oral History Archive, preserving multilingual accounts of migration, labor, farming, and everyday civic life.

Learning Outcomes

  • Design an ethical interview protocol for sensitive regional histories.
  • Connect oral testimony with archive material and public interpretation.
  • Produce a respectful public history object with participant consent.

Chronological Syllabus (4-week intensive modules)

  1. 01
    Ethnographic Listening and Consent

    Interview roles, consent language, positionality, and field note practice.

  2. 02
    Archives, Memory, and Minority Languages

    Working with multilingual sources and fragmented public records.

  3. 03
    Interpretation Without Extraction

    Participant review, anonymization, translation limits, and narrative responsibility.

  4. 04
    Public History Studio

    A short exhibit script, audio excerpt plan, and ethics appendix.

Assessment Pattern

Interview protocol 25%, archive note 20%, field reflection 25%, public history object 30%.

Lab Crucible
Dashboard Usability Quota

Academic Lab Affiliate
Civic Data Interface Lab

Research Connection

Builds directly on PU's Civic Data Interface Lab research into accessible dashboards for municipal water, heat, and service access.

Learning Outcomes

  • Build a reproducible data pipeline with source notes and limitations.
  • Design an accessible public dashboard for non-specialist residents.
  • Test visual explanations with real users and revise based on evidence.

Chronological Syllabus (4-week intensive modules)

  1. 01
    Reproducible Notebook Foundations

    Data provenance, cleaning logs, joins, and versioned assumptions.

  2. 02
    Geospatial Interface Patterns

    Maps, tables, filters, legends, and mobile-first public access.

  3. 03
    Uncertainty and Accessibility

    Plain-language uncertainty, contrast, keyboard access, and screen-reader labels.

  4. 04
    Resident Test and Revision

    Usability sessions, issue triage, and a published dashboard release note.

Assessment Pattern

Notebook 25%, dashboard prototype 35%, user test report 20%, release note 20%.

Academic Lab Affiliate
Municipal Resilience Clinic

Research Connection

Works with partner municipalities to test how climate, infrastructure, and service-access evidence can be used in local planning cycles.

Learning Outcomes

  • Translate technical evidence into policy options for municipal partners.
  • Compare heat, flood, water, and access risks across neighborhoods.
  • Run a partner briefing that separates evidence, judgment, and recommendation.

Chronological Syllabus (4-week intensive modules)

  1. 01
    Partner Problem Intake

    Scope, stakeholders, available evidence, decision calendar, and constraints.

  2. 02
    Risk Comparison Framework

    Heat, flood, water, and access indicators with neighborhood-level caveats.

  3. 03
    Policy Option Design

    Tradeoffs, implementation cost, legal fit, and accountability measures.

  4. 04
    Partner Briefing

    A live briefing deck, evidence appendix, and follow-up action log.

Assessment Pattern

Partner intake 20%, risk framework 25%, policy brief 35%, live briefing 20%.

Capstone Evidence Dossier

Credits
10 ECTS
Academic Level
Partner Studio
Public Portfolio Standard
1 reviewed dossier
Academic Lab Affiliate
Applied Research Commons

Research Connection

The capstone converts a PU research or partner question into a documented public dossier with method transparency and stakeholder response.

Learning Outcomes

  • Frame a research question that can survive methodological review.
  • Produce a public dossier with data, methods, limitations, and prototype evidence.
  • Respond to partner critique with a revised, accountable final submission.

Chronological Syllabus (4-week intensive modules)

  1. 01
    Question and Method Gate

    Research question, partner need, data access, ethics, and feasibility review.

  2. 02
    Dataset and Prototype Sprint

    Evidence assembly, prototype build, testing plan, and limitation log.

  3. 03
    Partner Response Cycle

    Critique session, revision priorities, and public communication choices.

  4. 04
    Final Dossier Defense

    Public portfolio, methods appendix, partner response, and oral defense.

Assessment Pattern

Proposal gate 15%, dataset/prototype 35%, public dossier 35%, defense 15%.
